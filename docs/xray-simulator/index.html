How to use

3D setup — drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The beam pyramid shows the divergent X-rays from the focal spot to the detector; the detector shows the current image.

Radiograph — computed live by sending X-rays through a real CT scan. Mouse wheel zooms, drag pans, right-drag changes window (left/right) and level (up/down), double-click resets.

Projection & geometry — switch between PA and lateral; under Geometry & exposure change the beam direction (PA/AP), the source-image distance, rotate or tilt the patient, angle the tube or collimate the field. Watch the magnification and CTR below the image.

Pathology — add a nodule by clicking into a lung in 3D (then drag it to move it), or add consolidation, lobar atelectasis, pleural effusion and pneumothorax; Lines & tubes adds an endotracheal tube, nasogastric tube, central line or ECG leads. Mark findings (M), Lobes and Heart colour-code what projects where.

Quiz mode — random cases: name the finding, click its location, then see the answer.

Simulated images for education only, not for diagnosis. CT data: STOIC2021 (CC BY-NC 4.0). · Impressum · Datenschutz

Sources and licences

The radiographs are simulated from a real CT scan. All pathologies, lines and tubes are added by the simulator; they are not present in the original data.

CT data

  1. CT scan: case 2865 of STOIC2021 Training (chest CT of a patient examined for suspected COVID-19 pneumonia, France 2020; selected because it shows no lung pathology). Radboud University Medical Center. registry.opendata.aws/stoic2021-training, accessed 17 Oct 2026. Licence: CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial use only).
  2. Revel MP, Boussouar S, de Margerie-Mellon C, et al. Study of Thoracic CT in COVID-19: The STOIC Project. Radiology 2021;301(1):E361–E370. doi:10.1148/radiol.2021210384

Changes to the CT data: resampled and cropped to the chest, CT numbers rounded to multiples of 4 HU, arms removed from the field, organ and lobe segmentation, derived surface meshes and landmarks.
